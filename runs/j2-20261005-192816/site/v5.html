Simple daily habits that support a healthy heart

Sample post. Author: sample author, not a real CityCare doctor.

Looking after your heart does not need big changes. Small habits, repeated every day, add up over the years. Here are five that most people can start this week.

1. Move a little every day

A brisk walk on most days of the week is a good place to start. If you are new to exercise, begin with short walks and add time slowly.

2. Eat more plants, less salt

  • Fill half your plate with vegetables and fruit.
  • Choose whole grains and pulses more often.
  • Cut back on added salt, packaged snacks and deep-fried food.

3. Sleep and rest

Regular sleep helps your body manage blood pressure and stress. Try to keep a steady bedtime and wake-up time.

4. Avoid tobacco

If you smoke or chew tobacco, ask a doctor about ways to stop. Quitting helps at any age.

5. Get regular check-ups

Blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol often have no symptoms. A routine check-up is the only way to know your numbers, especially if heart disease or diabetes runs in your family.

When to get urgent help: chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, sudden weakness on one side of the body, or fainting. Call 108 or 112 straight away. Do not wait for an online reply.
