# Brew Lab

## Problem

Brew Lab is a small specialty coffee shop in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. People cannot see the menu or order ahead.

Coffee lovers and office staff nearby cannot look up what Brew Lab offers and what it costs before they visit, and they cannot ask for a coffee in advance to pick up at the shop.

## Users

The two user groups are coffee lovers and office staff nearby. Both are near the Brew Lab shop in Indiranagar, Bengaluru.

**Coffee lovers**

- See the menu with prices.
- See the opening hours.
- Find where the shop is.
- Pre-order a coffee for pickup.

**Office staff nearby**

- See the menu with prices.
- See the opening hours.
- Find where the shop is.
- Pre-order a coffee for pickup.

Both groups pre-order without paying online.

## Goals

The aim is a one-page, phone-friendly website for Brew Lab, a specialty coffee shop in Indiranagar, Bengaluru.

- **G1**: Give Brew Lab in Indiranagar, Bengaluru a one-page site that works on phones, where visitors see the menu with prices and pre-order a coffee for pickup without paying online.

## Requirements

These requirements apply to coffee lovers and office staff nearby, who both use the Brew Lab site.

1. **R1**: The page shows the menu with prices, so coffee lovers and office staff nearby can see each item and what it costs before they visit.
2. **R2**: The page has a pre-order form for pickup, so coffee lovers and office staff nearby can pre-order a coffee and collect it at the shop.
3. **R3**: The page shows the shop's map or address, so visitors can find where Brew Lab is in Indiranagar, Bengaluru.
4. **R4**: The page shows the opening hours, so visitors can check when Brew Lab is open without leaving the page.
5. **R5**: The site is one page only: the menu, pre-order form, map or address and opening hours are all on that single page.
6. **R6**: The site works on phones: a visitor on a phone can read the menu with prices, the opening hours and the address, and can fill in and send the pre-order form.
7. **R7**: The site has no payment: the pre-order form does not ask for or take any online payment.

## Success metrics

The site is working when visitors use it to pre-order coffee at Brew Lab. The target is counted every week.

| Goal | Metric |
|------|--------|
| G1 | M1: 30 pre-orders per week, sent through the pre-order form on the Brew Lab site (measurement window: one week) |

- **G1**: M1 measures this goal, because a pre-order sent through the form shows a visitor saw the menu with prices and ordered a coffee for pickup without paying online.
