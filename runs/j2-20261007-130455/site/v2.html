Our farms

The story behind our coffee, from three farms in Chikmagalur and Coorg.

Ratnagiri Estate

Chikmagalur

Ratnagiri Estate is a family estate. It grows its Arabica in the shade, at an altitude of 1,400 m.

The estate is known for its natural-process lots, which have fruity notes.

  • Family estate
  • Shade-grown Arabica
  • 1,400 m
  • Natural-process lots with fruity notes

Kerehaklu

Chikmagalur

Kerehaklu is a fourth-generation farm. It replants native forest trees on the land.

Coffee here grows alongside pepper and cardamom.

  • Fourth-generation farm
  • Replants native forest trees
  • Coffee grown alongside pepper and cardamom

Baarbara Estate

Coorg

Baarbara Estate is a small estate. Only ripe cherries are hand-picked.

The cherries are then dried slowly on raised beds.

  • Small estate
  • Only ripe cherries, hand-picked
  • Dried slowly on raised beds
