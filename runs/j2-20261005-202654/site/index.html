Patient FAQs

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the request form on this page and send it on WhatsApp or by email. The clinic will confirm your appointment by phone. The form is a request and does not guarantee a slot until the clinic calls you.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. Prices are starting prices only. The final cost depends on your teeth and the treatment you need, and it is confirmed after examination.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Use the emergency contact at the top of this page and call, rather than sending the appointment form. For trouble breathing or swallowing, or bleeding that will not stop, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

What should I do if a tooth is knocked out?

Pick it up by the crown (the white part), not the root. Do not scrub it. Keep it moist, for example in milk, and call the emergency number straight away, because a quick visit gives the best chance of saving the tooth.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a list of any medicines you take, details of any medical conditions or allergies, and any earlier dental X-rays or reports you have.
