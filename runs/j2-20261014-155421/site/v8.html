FAQs

Quick answers to the questions runners ask most.

Can I change my race?

Yes, until 31 January 2027 by email.

Is there parking?

Yes, free at Gate 2.

Are refunds available?

No.

Where do I collect my bib?

At the expo on 13 February.

Is there a bag drop?

Yes, near the start line.

Are pacers provided?

Yes, for the 10K and Half Marathon.
